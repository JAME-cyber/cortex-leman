# Distribution Strategy for FR-CH B2B — Page-Theme Analysis

> Source: Greg Isenberg tweet (@startupideaspod, 7 Aug 2026) → strategy adaptation → GPT-5.6 cross-validation via OpenRouter.
> Lesson: the "anonymous topic page" play from US creators does not directly transfer to FR-CH B2B.

## The Source Concept (Greg Isenberg / Julian Shapiro)

- 1000 LinkedIn impressions cost ~$22 paid.
- An account with 500 followers can clear 1000 impressions organically on a single post.
- Julian Shapiro ran a "Growth Tactics" X page (not branded as Demand Curve) → followers interested in the topic → discovered his agency.
- "Every company has a topic its buyers already follow. Own that page."

## Why It Doesn't Transfer 1:1 to FR-CH

| Factor | US market | FR-CH market |
|---|---|---|
| Market size | Millions of B2B buyers | Small, relationship-driven |
| Trust model | Content authority scales | Requires human face + local credibility |
| Anonymous pages | Work at scale | Breed suspicion (who is responsible?) |
| Distribution | X/YouTube algorithmic reach | LinkedIn personal profile = primary channel |
| Audience quality | Volume compensates for noise | Must be ICP-qualified from day 1 |

## Corrected Strategy (GPT-5.6 Recommendations)

### Single media, signed, not anonymous

- **Primary channel:** Founder's personal LinkedIn profile (not a page)
- **Newsletter:** LinkedIn newsletter (bi-weekly) — owned subscription
- **Landing/email:** Cortex Leman site + email list (truly owned audience)
- **Attribution:** Transparent — "Média édité par Cortex Leman"
- **NO anonymous topic accounts** — the FR-CH market requires knowing who speaks

### One theme, one offer, 90 days

- **Theme:** "IA opérationnelle et gouvernée pour PME romandes" (NOT "Conformité IA" — PMEs don't buy compliance, they buy less risk + faster decisions)
- **Offer:** "Diagnostic IA & données" (NOT "Audit" — see pitfall #8)
- **Metrics:** 10-15 conversations with ICP, 2-3 paid diagnostics, 1 upsell — NOT follower count

### 4 editorial pillars

1. **Cas d'usage opérationnels** — concrete workflows (sales qualification, proposal prep, document processing)
2. **Gouvernance et données** — nLPD, vendor assessment, internal policies, data transfers
3. **Tests et benchmarks** — cloud vs self-hosted, real costs, accuracy, time saved
4. **Retours terrain** — before/after, errors, limits, when automation isn't worth it

### Compliance triad (corrected)

Core: **nLPD + RGPD (when EU-facing) + AI Act**
Vertical-specific only: MiCA (crypto/fintech), ZertES (e-signatures)

## Key Correction: The $22/1000 Impression Math

This metric is **not a valid FR-CH economic justification**:
- Paid impressions are targeted (role, sector, location, seniority); organic impressions are not equivalent
- 500 followers ≠ guaranteed 1000 impressions (depends on engagement, format, algorithm)
- Organic content is not free (research, writing, validation, design, distribution time)
- FR-CH LinkedIn CPMs are higher but inventory is limited

**Better metrics:**
- CAC contenu = (temps + outils + production) / clients attribuables
- Valeur éditoriale = marge du pipeline influencé − coût total de production
- Share of ICP readers vs total audience

## Recommended Offer Naming

| Don't say | Say instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Audit conformité | Diagnostic de gouvernance IA | "Audit" implies legal responsibility + opposable report |
| Self-hosted souverain | Self-hosted (with demonstrated data-flow) | "Souverain" is contestable without proof |
| Conformité IA CH | IA opérationnelle et gouvernée | PMEs buy results, not compliance |
| PRISME (to clients) | La méthode derrière les résultats | Method names are for internal/positioning, not cold pitches |
