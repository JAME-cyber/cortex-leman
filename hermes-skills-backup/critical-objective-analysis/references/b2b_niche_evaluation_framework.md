# B2B Niche Evaluation: 3 Pillars × 4 Criteria (Naier Saidane Framework)

**Source**: Naier Saidane, "0 Client, je Répare ce qui l'Empêche de Signer en 30 Minutes" (YouTube, Jul 2026)
**Verified**: Applied to Thierry's restaurant vertical (Menuo + Voice Agent) — passed all 4 criteria.

---

## The 3 Pillars of B2B Prospecting

When B2B prospecting yields zero results, the reflex is to fix what's visible (tools, volume, email templates). But prospecting = **Cible × Message × Infrastructure**. If ANY pillar fails, results are zero — regardless of the other two.

```
Cible (WHO)  ×  Message (WHAT you say)  ×  Infrastructure (HOW it arrives)
```

**Diagnosis order**: Always start with Cible → Message → Infrastructure. Fixing infrastructure on a broken target is wasted effort.

### Pillar 1: Infrastructure (eliminate first)

Check these before blaming tools:
- Bounce rate: <5% acceptable, target 2-3%
- Email health: 95-100% deliverability
- Domain/box rotation: 3+ domains, 9+ mailboxes
- If you're getting ANY replies (even "out of office"), emails ARE landing — infrastructure is NOT the problem

**Verdict pattern**: If infra is clean AND you have zero positive replies → problem is in Cible or Message, NOT infra.

### Pillar 2: Cible (the usual killer)

A niche must pass **all 4 criteria** at ≥6/10. A single 1-2/10 kills the entire niche regardless of how well the other 3 score.

#### The 4 Criteria Test

| Criterion | Question | Scoring |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **ROI** | Does your offer make the prospect money or save time — directly and visibly? | 10 = direct revenue/efficiency gain they feel in their bank account/calendar |
| **Marché** | Are prospects reachable cold and accustomed to buying new tools? | 10 = active on LinkedIn/email, buy software regularly |
| **LTV** | Is one client worth ≥1 000€ over the relationship? | 10 = clearly above 1K€ LTV |
| **Régulation** | Is the buying process light (no security committees, DSI validation, external audits)? | 10 = founder/owner decides alone, same-week decision possible |

**Critical rule**: A single score ≤2/10 on ANY criterion = niche killed. No amount of message optimization saves a regulated sector or an invisible ROI.

#### Common failure patterns

- **Finance/Insurance**: Régulation = 1/10 (DSI + security committee + external audit). Kills everything.
- **"Data quality"/"dashboard reliability"** offers: ROI = 2/10 (indirect benefit, nobody feels it in their bank account).
- **Too-wide target** (50-500 employees): CEO at 50 ≠ CEO at 500. Different jobs, different pain, same generic message = resonates with nobody.
- **Over-solicitation**: 61 contacts at the same company burns reputation before the conversation starts.

### Pillar 3: Message (only fix after Cible is validated)

When the target is right, the message writes itself — you know the exact pain, the exact decision-maker, the exact result they want.

#### Email anatomy (4 blocks)

| Block | Purpose | Score killer |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| **Objet** | Look like an internal email, not marketing | Questions marks, marketing words = 3/10 |
| **Personnalisation** | Show you know their daily reality | "Semble" / generic phrases = 2/10 |
| **Douleur + Crédibilité** | Name their pain + your relevant experience | Wrong reality (BI team they don't have) = 2/10 |
| **Offre + Garantie** | Sell a RESULT not a diagnostic; guarantee in the right direction | "I'll find problems" is scary = 3/10 |
| **CTA** | Minimal friction | "15 min call?" = 5/10. "Reply yes, I send a 2-min demo" = 8/10 |

#### 2026 outreach rules
- Max 2 follow-ups (3 days apart). More = exasperation.
- 3-month re-contact cycle after exhausting a list.
- Summer (Jul-Aug) is GOOD for B2B: less client work = more time for structural improvements.
- "IA" in the offer: for traditional niches (accountants, DAF), IA is a black box = anxiety. Mention only if you reassure that humans keep validation control.

## Application Example: Restaurant Vertical (Jul 2026)

Applied to Menuo + Voice Agent targeting independent restaurants in Haute-Savoie (74) / Ain (01).

| Criterion | Score | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **ROI** | 8/10 | Missed call = lost order = direct revenue loss. Visibly felt. |
| **Marché** | 6/10 | Not on LinkedIn, but reachable via phone + walk-in + Instagram. Lower than B2B tech. |
| **LTV** | 7/10 | 299€/mois × 12 = 3 588€/an. Above 1K€ threshold. |
| **Régulation** | 7/10 | No DSI committee. Owner/gérant decides alone. Same-week decision possible. |

**Verdict**: All 4 criteria ≥6/10. Niche passes. No single criterion is fatal.

**Contrast**: Mohamed's original niche (insurance/finance, 50-500 employees) had Régulation = 1/10 → killed despite 8-9/10 on other criteria.

### Sub-niche segmentation (Naier's approach)

Don't send one message to a broad niche. Segment into 3 sub-niches with distinct pain + offer + message:

| Sub-niche | Pain | Offer | ROI | Régul |
|-----------|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Pizzerias/Snacks | Missed calls during rush = lost orders | Voice Agent saves orders | 9 | 8 |
| Grills/Brasseries | Lost weekend reservations | Voice Agent + QR booking | 7 | 7 |
| Traiteurs/Events | Unanswered quote calls during service | Voice Agent captures requests | 7 | 7 |

Run all 3 in parallel. Let the market tell you which converts best — don't predict, test.

## When to Use This Framework

- Evaluating a new B2B niche before investing in prospecting
- Diagnosing why current prospecting yields zero results
- Deciding whether to pivot target or pivot message
- Segmenting a broad niche into actionable sub-niches
- Preparing counter-analysis inputs (pair with multi-model counter-analysis for validation)

## When NOT to Use

- B2C (consumer) markets — the 4 criteria assume B2B buying processes
- Markets where you already have traction (the framework is diagnostic, not optimization)
- Trivial targeting decisions (one segment, obvious fit)
