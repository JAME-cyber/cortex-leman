## Key Method: "Vendre avant de faire"

Clément's core thesis: **Don't pick a niche first. Pick an acquisition system first.**

1. Choose HOW you'll find clients (mailing, LinkedIn, content, ads, webinars, challenges, VSL funnels)
2. The acquisition method determines which niches are reachable
3. Only THEN specialize — because "all niches can make money"
4. Always sell before building — validate demand with a paid call

**7+ acquisition channels listed:** mailing masse, mailing ultra-personnalisé, LinkedIn masse, LinkedIn perso, création de contenu (multi-platform), cold calling, ads (Meta/LinkedIn/TikTok/Google), SEO, VSL tunnels, webinaires, challenges

## Demo Workflow: Automated Proposal Generation

Their flagship demo at HEC = n8n workflow that generates a complete commercial proposal in ~5 min (vs 6-8h manual):

1. **Call recording** → transcript
2. **Claude** extracts: context, client needs, budget figures, pain points (verbatim quotes)
3. **Synthesis** fed into **Gamma** (AI presentation tool) to generate branded proposal doc
4. **PandaDoc** or similar for e-signature + legal contract
5. **Email** sent automatically with proposal + invoice

**Key insight**: They extract the client's exact words ("ça devrait être conforme RGPD") and mirror them back in the proposal. The client sees their own problem described verbatim = instant trust.

## Competitive Divergence vs Cortex Leman

### What ZénithIA does NOT have (our advantages)

1. **RGPD-IA combined positioning** — Nobody in FR-CH does this. Classic auditeurs know RGPD but not AI. AI agencies know automation but not compliance.
2. **Recurring revenue model** — ZénithIA sells projects (boom-bust). Our CHF 500-1.5K/month conformity subscription = predictable income.
3. **Swiss data sovereignty** — n8n self-hosted in CH > cloud US. PME romandes care deeply about this. ZénithIA can't copy this without CH presence.
4. **Quality-over-volume acquisition** — SocialPulse scoring > 300 mails/day. In B2B CH, a CFO won't respond to mass mailing.
5. **FR-CH legal expertise** — LPerD + EU AI Act + RGPD intersection. ZénithIA operates FR-only, no Swiss law capability.

### What ZénithIA does better (to copy)

1. **Automated proposal generation workflow** — Copy their n8n + Claude + Gamma pipeline for audit report generation. Reduces delivery time from 2 days to 15 min.
2. **"Mirror the client's words" technique** — Extract verbatim pain points from calls and reflect them in proposals. Universal trust builder.
3. **Content → community → B2B funnel** — YouTube → private community → enterprise audits. Consider this for Cortex Leman content strategy.
4. **3-tier positioning clarity** — Simple/medium/complex is easy for prospects to understand. Mirror this in our Diagnostic/Audit/Conformité tiers.
