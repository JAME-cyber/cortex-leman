# Hybrid Marketing Package Creation

## Overview

Create comprehensive marketing packages with **hybrid positioning** (technical USP + business value proposition) following a systematic three-step structure.

**Purpose:** Balance technical credibility (OWASP standards, frameworks) with business justification (pricing, timeline, ROI) for B2B audiences.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this approach when you have:
1. A technical USP that needs business justification
2. Target audience includes both technical and business decision-makers
3. Need consistent messaging across multiple formats
4. Differentiation involves multiple value propositions

---

## Core Concept

### Hybrid Positioning = 50% Technical + 50% Business

**Three-Layer Structure:**
- **Layer 1: Technical Proof** - OWASP standards, frameworks, methodologies
- **Layer 2: Economic Justification** - Pricing, timeline, expertise, ROI
- **Layer 3: Success Guarantee** - Compliance, support, revisions

**Why Hybrid?**
- Technical audiences need proof of expertise
- Business audiences need ROI and budget justification
- Pure technical messaging alienates business decision-makers
- Pure business messaging lacks technical credibility

---

## Step 1: Determine Strategy

**Three Questions:**
1. What is your technical USP? (OWASP, frameworks, certifications)
2. What is your business differentiator? (Pricing, timeline, expertise, risk)
3. Who are your targets? (Technical vs Business)

**Decision Rule:**
- Pure technical: If target is 100% technical
- Pure business: If target is 100% business
- Hybrid (50/50): If target includes BOTH technical and business stakeholders

---

## Step 2: Define USP Layers

### Layer 1: Technical Proof

**Purpose:** Establish credibility with technical audiences

**Components:**
- OWASP standards (Top 10, ASVS, GenAI)
- Frameworks (phases, controls, methodologies)
- Comparisons vs competitors (technical superiority)

**Example:** "Premier audit à couvrir OWASP GenAI complet vs OneTrust/Vanta qui ne couvrent que OWASP Top 10"

### Layer 2: Economic Justification

**Purpose:** Justify investment for business audiences

**Components:**
- Pricing (one-time vs subscription)
- Timeline (delivery speed vs onboarding)
- Expertise (local support, language)
- ROI calculation (sanctions vs cost)

**Example:** "Prix 1500-7500€ one-time vs 5000-50000€ an | Délai 2-3 semaines vs 6-12 mois | Expertise locale FR-CH"

### Layer 3: Success Guarantee

**Purpose:** Remove risk and provide reassurance

**Components:**
- Compliance completeness
- Support during engagement
- Revisions and satisfaction guarantee
- Risk mitigation

**Example:** "Conformité complète (OWASP + AI Act + LPD + GDPR) | Support 2-3 semaines | Révisions incluses | ROI > 10x"

## File Content Guidelines

### Templates Marketing

**LinkedIn Template (4 versions, 10-11KB)**
- Version 1: Direct (technical + business balance)
- Version 2: Conversational (storytelling)
- Version 3: Short and direct
- Version 4: Business angle
- Hooks par secteur
- 3 follow-ups
- Réponses aux objections
- Instructions d'envoi

**Email Template (4 versions, 10-11KB)**
- Similar to LinkedIn but more detailed
- Include full comparison tables
- Thank you email post-meeting

**Fiche Produit (9-10KB)**
- Product overview
- 3 audits/services detailed
- Différenciation table (8+ criteria)
- Avantages concurrentiels
- Tarifs (Startup/Growth/Enterprise)
- Expertise locale
- Contact info

**Landing Page HTML (30-35KB)**
- Hero section (badge, headline, stats, CTAs)
- Technical section (Layer 1 USP)
- Pricing section (Layer 2 differentiators)
- Footer (contact)

### FAQ Hybrides

**FAQ Techniques (8-9KB)**
- Technical USP questions
- Related standards
- Compliance/regulations

**FAQ Business (9-10KB)**
- Pricing questions
- Timeline questions
- Expertise questions
- ROI questions
- Guarantee questions

**FAQ Conformité (8-9KB)**
- Regulation 1 (AI Act)
- Regulation 2 (LPD suisse)

**Slide 1: Differentiation (3-4KB)**

**Slide 3: Business Differentiators (5-6KB)**

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Technical-Only Positioning:** Use hybrid (50/50) with three-layer USP
2. **Inconsistent Messaging:** Define USP layers upfront, use consistently
3. **No ROI Calculation:** Calculate ROI (sanctions vs cost), include in materials
4. **No Comparison Tables:** Include 8+ criteria tables in Fiche, Landing Page, Deck
5. **Overcrowded Slides:** Focus on key points, use tables, split content

---

## Best Practices

### DO
- Balance technical depth with business justification
- Use consistent messaging across all materials
- Include comparison tables (8+ criteria)
- Provide ROI calculations
- Use three-layer USP structure
- Create reusable templates
- Structure files logically
- Include KPI targets

### DON'T
- Focus only on technical OR only on business
- Change messaging between materials
- Skip comparison tables
- Omit ROI calculations
- Create one-off materials
- Overcrowd slides
- Forget KPIs
