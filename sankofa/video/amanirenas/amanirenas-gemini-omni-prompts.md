**Deadline: 11 août 23:59 Pacific = 12 août 08:59 CET**

## Specs Gemini Omni
- Durée: clips ~8-15s
- Résolution: 1080p (4K disponible)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 ou 9:16
- Audio natif inclus (ambient + voix)
- Entrée: texte ou image de référence

## Structure du prompt
Chaque prompt suit: Subject → Environment → Camera → Lighting → Mood → Style → Quality

## SCÈNE 1 — Le royaume de Kush (establishing shot)
**Prompt:**
> Aerial establishing shot of the ancient city of Meroë, Kingdom of Kush, 25 BCE. Golden desert landscape with palm groves along the Nile. More than 200 steep pyramids — smaller and steeper than Egyptian pyramids — rise from the ochre earth. The Royal Palace stands at the city center, sandstone walls glowing in golden hour light. Camera slowly descends from high altitude to rooftop level, revealing the bustling city: merchants, soldiers in bronze armor, nobles in white linen robes. Dust particles catch the sunlight. Lighting: warm golden hour, deep amber tones. Mood: majestic, ancient, powerful. Style: cinematic historical documentary, photorealistic. Quality: 4K, ultra-detailed.

## SCÈNE 2 — Amanirenas, reine guerrière
**Prompt:**
> Medium portrait of Queen Amanirenas of Kush, a powerful Nubian woman in her 40s with dark skin, high cheekbones, a war scar across her left eye (she was one-eyed). She wears a golden crown in the Kushite style, gold earrings, and a white linen royal robe with gold embroidery. A bronze sword hangs at her side. She stands on the palace balcony overlooking Meroë, the Nile pyramids behind her. Her expression is fierce, determined, regal. Camera: slow push-in from medium shot to close-up. Lighting: dramatic side lighting, deep shadows on the right half of her face. Mood: commanding, defiant, royal. Style: cinematic historical drama, photorealistic, volumetric light. Quality: 4K, ultra-detailed faces.

## SCÈNE 3 — L'armée kushite en marche
**Prompt:**
> Wide tracking shot of the Kushite army marching through the Nubian desert toward the Egyptian border, 25 BCE. Hundreds of soldiers in bronze armor with hide shields, archers with longbows, and cavalry on Nubian horses. Queen Amanirenas rides at the front on a white horse, one-eyed, golden crown glinting. A banner with the Kushite lion standard flutters in the wind. Dust rises from the marching column. Camera: low-angle tracking shot moving alongside the army at ground level, then craning up to reveal the massive column stretching to the horizon. Lighting: harsh midday desert sun, stark shadows. Mood: epic, militaristic, determined. Style: cinematic war epic, photorealistic. Quality: 4K, large-scale crowd detail.

## SCÈNE 4 — La bataille de Syène (Assouan)
**Prompt:**
> Epic battle scene at the Roman-Egyptian border town of Syène (Aswan), 25 BCE. Kushite warriors overwhelm Roman garrison soldiers in chaotic close combat. Kushite archers rain arrows from elevated positions. Bronze spears clash with Roman gladius swords. A bronze statue of Emperor Augustus is being toppled from its pedestal. Dust, smoke, and sand fill the air. Queen Amanirenas, one-eyed, fights in the forefront on horseback, her gold crown gleaming. Camera: dynamic handheld-style tracking through the battle, quick pans between combatants, then a dramatic slow-motion shot of the Augustus statue falling. Lighting: dusty haze filtering harsh sunlight, amber dust tones. Mood: chaotic, triumphant, violent. Style: cinematic battle scene, photorealistic. Quality: 4K, particle effects.

## Notes d'utilisation

1. **Priorité:** Scènes 2, 4, 7 = les plus importantes (portrait Amanirenas, bataille, résistance)
2. **Audio:** Laisser Gemini Omni générer l'audio natif — ambiance bataille, désert, foule
3. **Édition chat:** Après génération, essayer:
   - "Make the camera movement slower"
   - "Add more dust particles"
   - "Increase the golden hour warmth"
4. **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 pour le YouTube long-form, 9:16 si adapté en Short
5. **Identité Amanirenas:** TOUJOURS préciser "one-eyed, war scar, dark skin, high cheekbones, Kushite golden crown" pour maintenir la cohérence
6. **Limites:** Si Gemini refuse une scène de bataille (violence), adoucir: "Kushite soldiers clashing shields, dramatic tension without graphic violence"
